·ONE STRING, EIGHT FRETS

The eight frets below were written as one register per person, and they run in the order a family is actually met: father, mother, brother, the one holding him, the friend who untangled the knot, then three stanzas from a song that a district already knows by heart.

They sit inside a machine operator — FOLD(spherePop), a nine-step monozukuri pipeline — because the same law governs both: a thing is made by taking freedom away, step by step, never all at once. The raw cylinder can be reshaped. The laminated stack can be pressed but not split. The pressed stack can be drilled, the drilled one sealed, the sealed one trimmed, the trimmed one polished, the polished one only placed.

That is the only reason the eight voices can be printed beside a manufactured part without the page turning into a metaphor: commitment is attenuation, in wood and in a family both.

Each fret carries its stage of the fold and its trit. The three terms are constant throughout: −1 is a cut or a compression, 0 is a gate named as a boundary, +1 is something held.

✳THE EIGHT-FRET SEAL, RECOMPUTED

The source document carries a twelve-column consensus over the eight frets and seals it with sixteen bytes of hex. Both were recomputed.

The seal decodes exactly: TEAMLAB-VAKED-8B, sixteen bytes, byte for byte.

Under the formula the document actually prints — bar_p = softmax((1/K) Σ z_k / T) — the collapse is arithmetic-mean then softmax, every column mean is positive, and the row comes out + + + + + + + + + + + +. The claim reproduces.

The word beside that formula is “geometric-mean consensus”, and a geometric mean of signed trits is the sign of their product. Five columns contain a −1, and any 0 kills a product outright; under a true geometric mean nine of the twelve columns collapse to 0, giving 0 0 0 0 0 + 0 0 0 + 0 +. So the number is right and the operation is misnamed — the same species of error as a tolerance printed an order of magnitude above its own measurement: the measurement was honest, the label was not.

Kept here rather than corrected in place, because the row itself is evidence that the consensus was computed under the printed formula, and the misnaming is evidence that nobody re-derived the label after choosing the operation.
