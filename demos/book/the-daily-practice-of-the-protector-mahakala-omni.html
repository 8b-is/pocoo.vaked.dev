1. What a Pecha Even Is (དཔེ་ཆ་)

A traditional Tibetan book of loose horizontal pages wrapped in cloth. These 81 folios are a daily liturgy: a fixed fold reopened every morning, not a book read once.

2. The Three-Word Engine (-1 → 0 → +1)

MA DAK NÖ CHÜ (impure cup & contents, -1) → TONG PAR JANG (purified into emptiness, 0) → OM AH HUNG (blessed & offered as wisdom light, +1). You never skip the zero.

3. Amrita · Torma · Serkyem & Receipts

Amrita = blessed nectar (five poisons transmuted); Torma = sculpted offering cake; Serkyem = golden libation tea. Every folio names its lineage author (8th, 9th, 13th, 14th, 16th Karmapas) as a cryptographic provenance receipt.
