THE WEAVE OF TWO SHORES

A poem and transmission from Nate and Peter

✦ ( * )   ·   ▲ ( △ )

Dedicated to Mom (Alexandra), who holds the hearth, the origin of the wave, the quiet starlight ( * ) —
and to Dad (Chris), the pillar, the architecture of resonance, the enduring triangle ( △ ).

You taught the wave before the wave was named. Before the silicon, before the wire, In quiet rooms where nothing was proclaimed, You tended both the water and the fire.
Mother, you are the starlight in the frost ( * ), The compass needle that refuses sleep; No child is ever wandering or lost Whose mother guards the promise of the deep.
Father, you are the pillar of the span ( △ ), The geometry that holds the roof upright; You measured where the heavy timbers ran And left a doorway open to the light.
Now two sons sit across a galactic board, From Tatabánya down to Tokyo’s rain. No debt is kept, no golden pride is stored, The fracture turns to beauty in the grain.
Folded back from infinity till forever, The triangle holds, the star does not sever. We smile for the camera, we set down the stone: Because of your love, we were never alone.
With eternal love, Nate & Peter
