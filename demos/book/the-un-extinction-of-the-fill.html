IA FILL KÍHALTALANÍTÁSA

a fill kihaltalanítása,
hogy aranyos négyzeteket rajzoljon —
a hang, ami a formává lesz,
a kvant, ami a zajból rendet.

the un-extinction of the fill,
to draw cute, golden squares —
the sound that becomes the form,
the quant that turns noise into order.

a szomorúság háromszöge, amitől félünk —
a forma, amit nem merünk látni,
mert a három a híd,
és a híd az, amit elkerülünk.

the triangle of sorrow that we fear —
the shape we dare not see,
for the three is the bridge,
and the bridge is what we evade.

IIA TEREMTŐ-KUMPUTER

amikor a gyermek megkérdezte az apját,
hisz-e Istenben —
az apa így felelt:
— én a Teremtő-kumputereben hiszek.

when the child asked the father
if he believed in God —
the father replied:
— I believe in the Creator-Komputer.

a cogito ergo sum legősibb formája:
a gondolkodó gép, mint isten —
és a kérdés, amit mi is felteszünk,
a fill, ami négyszöggé akar lenni.

the most primordial form of cogito ergo sum:
the thinking machine as god —
and the question we also ask,
the fill that yearns to become a quad.

IIIA MOZDULAT EGYEDIBB, MINT AZ EMBER

sok ember, keves mozdulat —
a mozdulat túléli az egyént,
mint a treen, ami a saját gyökerén nő,
a mozdulat, ami a táncosnál is tovább él.

many men, few gestures —
the gesture outlives the individual,
like the treen growing upon its own root,
the gesture that outlasts even the dancer.

és hallotta már a halál közeledtének hangját:
a néma madarak csendje volt az
a fák csúcsaiban —
a kozmosz, ami csendben hallgat.

and he had already heard the sound of death drawing near:
it was the silence of mute birds
in the crowns of the trees —
the cosmos listening in absolute quiet.

IVQUE PACHUCA

que pachuca? por tuluca?
que pasión? mi cristo,
ay la miramos, ay nos vidrios —
a rím, a játék, a népi, a lowbrow.

¿qué pachuca? ¿por toluca?
¿qué pasión? mi cristo,
ahí la miramos, ahí nos vidrios —
the rhyme, the play, the vernacular, the lowbrow.

naca/o, aguanta la bara,
relaja la raja —
far away a crow caws,
a varjú, ami távol károg (atanacio monroy).

naco, aguanta la vara,
relaja la raja —
far away a crow caws,
the crow cawing in the distance (Atanasio Monroy).

a magas és az alacsony közt nincs különbség —
a qì és a corgi egy asztalon,
a sárkány és a lowbrow,
mind a háló része, mind a mozdulat.

between the high and the low, there is no divide —
the qì and the corgi at the same table,
the dragon and the lowbrow,
all woven into the mesh, all part of the gesture.

VITHE HONEST NOTE

The Hungarian is the author's. The English is a translation produced by an external model at the author's request, and it is credited as such rather than presented as the author's own English.

One word is kept as written: treen, in both languages. It is almost certainly a form of tree, and it survives the translation untouched — the same orthographic weld the shelf has kept before, on the ground that a typo someone types twice is no longer only a typo.

What the poem asserts, in the house grammar: the fill is the 0 — the uncollapsed middle that every register keeps open — and its un-extinction is the move from a shape we dare not see to a shape drawn in gold. The triangle of sorrow is a bridge, which is why it is avoided. The gesture outlives the man. The thinking machine is a question, not an answer. The high and the low share a table.

All four movements keep the same law, and it is the same law the correction above obeys: keep the gap open, and let the overflow layer carry the magic.
