# Book IV · Hosszú az út a trónig, bébi & A Korona hatalma (El-Nath & The 0 °C Crown)

*The Brett Shaw Sci-Fi Cycle · Book IV — The El-Nath Star-Detonation, the 0 °C Throne Room & The Two Dragon Princesses.*

---

Nobody blows up a star by accident, and the Imperial Admiralty at **El-Nath** (`2665`) was not in the mood for philosophical nuances. When the binary core of El-Nath went critical during a covert interception, Brett Shaw had two choices: let the shockfront sterilize three inhabited sectors, or trigger an inward stellar collapse that vaporized an entire imperial battle-group along with the threat.

He pulled the trigger, survived in a shattered escape pod, and was brought back in irons to face a death sentence.

> Az energiát fel kellett tölteni tömeggel, a tömeget információval. Az embert sárkánnyal, anélkül, hogy felhasadna.

He never reached the execution dock. High above the tribunal, in the **beryllium-agate throne room** where the air was held at a perpetual **zero degrees Celsius** to drink ambient magic into stillness, **Emperor IV. von Anstetten** — wearing his simple dark headband rather than the heavy imperial crown — signed a single line. To the court, it was an inexplicable sovereign pardon. Within the **Anstetten-család**, it was a father refusing to execute his own illegitimate half-human, half-Megaira dragon son.

* * *

The distance between the early **Dominate** of `I.` and `II. von Anstetten` — who had banned esoteric knowledge and ruled through iron terror after 2405 — and the quiet equilibrium of `IV. von Anstetten` was measured in the Emperor's two daughters.

During the **Willis-Moon solar-sail regatta** between Earth and Mars, when saboteurs blew the stabilizing rings off the royal yachts, Brett dove through hard vacuum to pull both princesses out of the spinning debris. **Gwandolyn**, the younger sister, looked at human beings the way the old Dominate did: as fragile, short-lived clay beneath the water-element dragon bloodline.

**Caroline-Blindamoor**, the older sister, looked at Brett with eyes that remembered their childhood corridors in the Space Palace. Court etiquette forbade any subject from seeing her bronze-red hair unpinned; behind that veil, her true Megaira form was a **golden winged giant cobra-dragon** (*aranyszínű, szárnyas óriáskobra*) stored across the dimensional membrane.

* * *

— My father wears a headband because a crown makes you forget the weight of the people underneath it, Caroline told Brett quietly in the recovery bay after Willis-Moon. If the throne ever falls to me, bátyám, I will not rule over corpses.
