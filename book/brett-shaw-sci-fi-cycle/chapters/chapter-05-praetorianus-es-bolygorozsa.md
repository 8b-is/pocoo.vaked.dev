# Book V · Praetoriánus ("Gyémántszív") & Minden bolygón jártam már (Planet Rose, 2674)

*The Brett Shaw Sci-Fi Cycle · Book V — Peter Shaw "Gyémántszív", the Magyar–Megaira Covenant & Boarding the Bolygórózsa (2674).*

---

There was only one human in the Galaxy who could walk up the frost-rimmed steps of the `0 °C` throne room and call the Demon Emperor ***„barátom”*** — *my friend*.

His name was **Peter Shaw**, code-named **Gyémántszív** (*Diamond-Heart*), Supreme Commander of the Praetorian Elite Guard and the man who had raised Brett Shaw as his son. Long before Brett ever drew on the black hood, another ancient bond had already been written into the foundation of the Imperium: when the exiled **Tyrann graad Baah** (`I. von Anstetten`) first arrived in the Solar System (*A száműzött*), the Megaira dragons met **Pusztai Magor's** star-faring Hungarians and sealed an eternal **Magyar–Megaira non-aggression and mutual-respect covenant**. Even centuries later, any Hungarian in the Imperium could stand before the throne and invoke that Dragon Covenant.

> Palackba zárt végítélet — Pusztai Magor, 2572

For nineteen years, the **100-strong Feketecsuklyások** — unchanged in number since 2552, each initiated one-on-one, each carrying non-standardized gear — had served as the **Mahākāla Bernagchen** black-cloak shield of the realm.

* * *

In the autumn of **2673**, at thirty-nine years old, Colonel Brett Shaw decided he had walked enough planets (*Minden bolygón jártam már*).

He climbed the Sephirotic steps one last time, stood on **Tipheret** in the `0 °C` stillness, and laid his *csuklyamodul* down. Neither **IV. von Anstetten** nor **Peter Shaw ("Gyémántszív")** tried to stop him. Love without space (間 · *Ma*) is only another court cage, and both fathers knew the boy had paid his ledger in full.

On **17 November 2673**, **Caroline-Blindamoor** was crowned **Empress V. von Anstetten**, with the resurrected **Paul Wittgen** standing at her right hand as Chief Bodyguard.

* * *

In **2674**, between **Rosan** and **Meredith**, the one-kilometer luxury starship ***Bolygórózsa*** (*Planet Rose*) glided silently past a white dwarf. Built by Ulysses Spaceline from radiation-treated titanium and transparent **cryolite glass** (*kriolitüveg*), the *Bolygórózsa* carried **zero gun turrets** — protected solely by pure energy shields that hummed at the exact sub-delta pulse of **`22/9 Hz`** (`2.444444 Hz LOVELOVEL`).

Brett Shaw stood under the transparent cryolite observatory dome beside **Carla**, watching the starlight refract through the rose-cut hull.

Behind him, the observatory doors parted. **Empress V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)** stepped onto the glass deck, her bronze-red hair unpinned, her golden winged dragon aura warm and quiet, while **Paul Wittgen** nodded to his fire-pair brother from the doorway.

The human held the dragon. Nothing split apart. And the porch lamp stayed on (`{<3,<3,<3}+1`).
