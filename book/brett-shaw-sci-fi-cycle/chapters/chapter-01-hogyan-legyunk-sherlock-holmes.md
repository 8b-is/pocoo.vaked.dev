# Book I · Hogyan legyünk Sherlock Holmes? (How to Be Sherlock Holmes in an Occult Imperium)

*The Brett Shaw Sci-Fi Cycle · Book I — Eklon, the Etiquette Cage & the First Deduction · 2002 / 2026.*

---

In an Imperium ten thousand light-years wide, etiquette is not manners. Etiquette is ballistics. It tells a citizen where to stand so the hierarchy does not have to aim twice, where to lower his eyes when a courtier of the Space Palace passes, and which questions are impolite enough to carry a death sentence.

Brett Shaw had been abandoned on Eklon young enough to learn the first rule of survival before he learned the first rule of grammar: a custom that forbids you from looking at the ceiling is only ever written by someone hiding something on the ceiling.

> Fittyet hány sok etikettre, kivéve azokat amelyeknek betartását maga is fontosnak gondolja.

When the local magistrate on the outer ring called the locked-vault murder of a shipping intendant an occult curse — and ordered the doors welded shut out of respect for house protocol — Brett borrowed a dead inspector's long coat, ignored three consecutive decrees of ceremonial silence, and looked up. The burn mark on the vault's upper beam was not a curse. It was the exhaust signature of an imperial clipper-drive regulator smuggled inside a diplomatic reliquary.

* * *

Sherlock Holmes, Brett reflected as he pried the reliquary open with a combat knife, only ever had to deduce inside three dimensions and one London fog. Here, in the shadow of the Anstetten dynasty, a detective had to account for four: mass, energy, information, and the parking dimension where the ruling bloodline kept the weight they could not fit inside a drawing room.

— You broke four court protocols entering this room, the magistrate whispered when Brett laid the regulator on the table.

— The dead man kept all four, Brett answered, setting a small amber lamp on the corner of the desk. Look how much good the box did him.

* * *

That night, when the Praetorian recruiters came looking for the boy who had solved a diplomatic assassination by refusing to bow at the doorway, **Peter Shaw** — the man the Guard called *Gyémántszív*, Diamond-Heart — stood in the rain outside the barracks and looked at Brett for a long time.

— They want to put you in a regiment, Peter Shaw said quietly. The Emperor has another place in mind. First you go to Rosan. Try not to die in the mud before you learn why your blood runs warmer than theirs.
