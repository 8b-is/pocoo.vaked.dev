The Brett Shaw Sci-Fi Cycle · Book III — Rosan IMC, Halmer's Zero-Sign Zone (2668) & Wittgen's Soul.

---

Years before the Black Hoods, in the freezing mud of the IMC training camp on Rosan, Private Brett Shaw should have died on an obstacle wire under live plasma fire. He hadn't, because a quiet recruit named Paul Wittgen had walked back into the kill-zone, hauled Brett over his shoulder, and carried him across the line without saying a word.

In 2668, on the methane-snow ridges of Halmer, the debt came due inside the Zero-Sign Zone (zérójelű zóna).

Egy vékony, zérójelű zóna vette körül a testet, mely mind a két típusú töltést vonzza és taszítja egyszerre.

Every csuklyamodul of the Feketecsuklyások was woven from Tartaros mist and linked across light-years to the mind of Emperor IV. von Anstetten. At rest, its thin zero-sign boundary held attraction (`+1`) and repulsion (`-1`) in simultaneous `Trit 0` equilibrium, shielding the wearer's luminous white soul cocoon (gubó) and spinal column. Its attack sensor had only one blind spot: a completely still or stopped heart.

∿

On Halmer, poisoned by the palace intrigues of Princess Gwandolyn, the remote imperial link severed mid-battle. The Emperor's consciousness departed Brett Shaw's hood without collapsing it.

Instantly, the csuklyamodul inverted into its `Trit -1` failure mode — palackba zárt végítélet, doomsday uncorked. Blind, autonomous, and lethal to anything with a beating heart, the hood locked Brett's cybernetic body into a berserk rampage while broadcasting a flare into the astral dark that drew the predator entity Monaterg.

The rest of the squad fell back. Paul Wittgen dropped his rifle in the snow and walked forward alone.

He did not fire on his brother. Stepping straight across the razor edge of the zero-sign perimeter, Wittgen locked his arms around Brett's shoulders to hold him until the hood's charge could spend itself. When Monaterg struck from the astral rift, killing Wittgen and attempting to tear into his corpse to wear it as a vessel, the temperature on the ridge dropped to zero degrees Celsius.

∿

IV. von Anstetten — the Demon Emperor himself — stood on the frozen basalt of Halmer. With a single gesture, he collapsed Brett's berserk hood back into silent cloth, crushed Monaterg's intrusion, and knelt beside the fallen captain. Reaching into the threshold before the silver cord could snap, the Emperor took Paul Wittgen's white soul cocoon and bound it back into his living chest.

When Brett opened his eyes in the snow, Wittgen was breathing beside him.
