Inside the Alef-osztag, nobody used radio chatter when Brett Shaw and Paul Wittgen took the point. Radio had latency; radio had encryption headers that could be stripped by an astral eavesdropper; radio required turning a thought into words and words back into movement.

Tökéletesen ismerik egymás gondolatait.

On the Kabbalistic stair of Initiation before the beryllium-agate throne, Wittgen had been raised in 2667 to Chokmah — the second Sephirah, the primordial flash of lightning before form — while Brett stood on Tipheret, the sixth Sephirah, the solar heart that holds the tree in balance. When they moved down a corridor together, Wittgen was the strike and Brett was the axis. Before Wittgen's boot touched the deck plate on Roxolán, Brett had already angled his black cloak three degrees left to catch the ricochet that hadn't been fired yet.

∿

Roxolán in 2669 smelled of sweet synthetic lotus smoke, overheated casinos, and infrared chandeliers (Szűz az infrafényben). Before combat drops, standard operatives — even Wittgen, when the old ghosts pressed too hard — took neural and virtual suppressants to iron every tremor of feeling out of the nervous system, turning the face into a stone mask (szobormerev arcú hadi gépezet). Only at the poker tables, when Wittgen lost a hand and left a hundred brawlers groaning in the wreckage of a Roxolán saloon, did the pressure valve blow.

Brett refused the ampoules.

Then came the night of the Pearl of Roxolán (Roxoláni Gyöngyszem). What began as a surgical extraction inside the orbital pleasure-dome spiraled into a crossfire of automated defense grids and collapsing pressure bulkheads. By the time the black hoods of the Alef-osztag stood in the smoking atrium, civilians who had only come to watch the dancers lay beneath the shattered cryolite glass.

∿

Back in the lower-ring bar, seventy percent of Brett's body — the trans-dimensional cybernetic ribs, the `Vajra Arm` anchored in the transfer plane — hummed without pain. It was the remaining thirty percent that ached until his hands shook on the glass.

Tubs slid a tumbler across the scarred zinc counter and did not offer a toast.

— The medics have a clean wipe for Roxolán, Tubs said. One ampoule and the Pearl is just coordinates in an after-action file.

Brett looked down at his cybernetic knuckles, then at the dark fabric of the hood folded beside his elbow.

Ha kitörlöm, Tubs, akkor már nem én hordom a csuklyát. Akkor a csuklya hord engem.
