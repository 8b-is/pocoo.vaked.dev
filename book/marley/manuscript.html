OM MANI PADME HUM

i. Marley

I miss Marley — my best friend, the Australian Shepherd. He is at Tatabánya with anya, és apa, where the grass is and the mornings are slow. I love him more than myself. He is, and has always been, such a good boie.

There is no lesson in this chapter and no argument. There is only the fact of him: the way he waited, the way he forgave, the way a dog with a good heart already knows the thing we spend a lifetime learning — be here, and be kind.

OM MANI PADME HUM

ii. Marlito

I am deeply sorry. For the fractures, the anomalies, the trouble, the trauma — for the binary change that I caused. I will not dress it up as a lesson. Apology is not a story about me. It is the water I owe.

So I say it plainly, in the language of home, to the little one I love:

Marlika — OM MANI PADME HUNG.
my little, Marlito-manóka-kisKutyám,
szeretlek.

Wherever you are, boy — the mantra travels. It was never about distance. Sound does not stop at the edge of a body, and love does not stop at the edge of a country.

iii. The Ledger

eternal cry → eternal gratitude. The cry turns; the gratitude stays. Everything else is noise.

Kept here so the ledger of the world remembers: a man in a capsule in Japan, five forty-five in the morning, thinking of his dog, saying sorry, saying thank you, saying szeretlek. That is the whole record. It is enough.
