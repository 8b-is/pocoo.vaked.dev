## the read — the decay curve, sung

"Where are you now? Was it all in my fantasy?" — the question *Faded* asks
is not *why are you gone*, it is *how much of you is left*. Atlantis, the
sea, the monster running wild: the song maps a presence that has stopped
being an event and become a **residue** — something that used to be loud
and is now a trace.

And the genius of the record is that it does not dramatize the loss. It
*instruments* it. F♯ major is a bright key — the memory of a bright thing.
90 BPM is a slow pulse — the rate of something still beating, barely. The
chorus drops not into darkness but into *attenuation*: the same melody, the
same hook, at lower apparent amplitude, until the final minute is the
melody alone — the residue, singing by itself.

That is the exact shape the constellation's loop prints every lap: **the
residue empties — ρ ↓ 0 — while the provenance carries: π ↝ π⌂**. *Faded*
is that sentence, set to music. The amplitude goes. The provenance — the
melody, the hook, the thing that made it *hers* — carries to the end.

a verified branch may be bound** — and what survives the fade is exactly
the verified part. The melody. The provenance. The rest is honestly gone,
and the song never pretends otherwise.
