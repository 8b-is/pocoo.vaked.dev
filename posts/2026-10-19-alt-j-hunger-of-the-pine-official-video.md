## the read

*Hunger Of The Pine* opens *This Is All Yours* (2014) after the *Intro* with a brass horn that sounds like a lighthouse through coastal fog, and then drops a Miley Cyrus sample (*"I'm a female rebel"*) right into the center of a quiet English pastoral chamber piece. Joe Newman compares missing someone to *"sleepless elite soldiers"* — an ache that stays awake on watch. When the album title says *This Is All Yours*, it hands the entire room over without condition.
