**Øostil & Juan Hansen — "Drown"**, remixed by Massano. Afterlife Records,
released 15 April 2022 on Massano's *In My System* EP, between the jagged
title track and the rest of the record. Melodic house & techno. **122 BPM ·

## the read — submersion as consent

Every track before this one negotiated at the surface. Drown is the first
one in the set that *agrees to go under* — not as loss, but as method. To
drown, in this song, is not to be defeated by the water; it is to stop
fighting it. The vocal doesn't plead. It reports. *Drown* — an imperative
spoken by someone who has already decided.

And here is where Massano's remix earns its name. A remix of *Drown* could
have pushed the descent further — deeper, darker, longer. Instead it does the
opposite thing, and that opposite thing **is** the resolution. The track's
key is D major: the brightest of the common techno keys, a key that does not
belong to a drowning. 122 BPM is not a storm, it is a *pressure* — the
Afterlife pace, heavy water that moves slowly and never lets you touch
bottom. For 7 minutes and 20 seconds the track holds you at the boundary,
and the drop — the one structure every club record is obligated to deliver —
arrives as the held breath, the moment the surface closes over, the
submersion made audible.

Then the resolution: the D major comes back up. **The remix does not drown
you. It drowns the original's despair and surfaces its consent.** The
descent was always the way through.

## the signal resolution

In the house ternary, the track reads as the full arc, in order:

| register | the signal | the ternary |
|---|---|---|
| the opening | the vocal states the fact — no plea, no fight | **−1** · the descent, accepted |
| the drop | the held breath — surface closed, pressure at maximum | **0** · the null between going down and coming up |
| the resolution | D major resurfaces — brighter than it left | **+1** · the surfacing, earned |

`−1 → 0 → +1` is not a melody structure; it is a *valence* structure, and it
is the exact shape the constellation's emotional ternary code was built to
carry. Most dance records run `0 → +1 → 0` — a peak you are given and then
gently returned. Drown inverts it: you are taken down first, *by consent*,
and the peak is not given, it is **surfaced into**. The negative is the
price of the positive. That is what makes the remix a *resolution* rather
than a decoration: it completes the arc the original could only start.

the track is not on the surface of the track. It is in the drop.** The
