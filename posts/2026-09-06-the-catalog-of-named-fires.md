# The catalog of named fires

## a SOTA dispatch · the state of the art, row by row

*the constellation · research · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*

---

> **What this is.** A new section of pocoo — *research* — for the state of
> the art as it actually stands: the open work, named. This first dispatch is
> the map. The next will be the epistemology that reads it.

There is a particular genre of engineering writing that reports *results*. This
is not that genre. This is the report of the **fires still burning** — the ones
we can name, rank, and assign a status, so that no thread is a vague promise
and no promise is mistakable for a fact.

The document at the center of it is the **WIP catalog** — contributed by Nate
(`@standardgalactic`), dated 2026-09-06. It is, by its own subtitle, "the source
of truth for the ongoing work-in-progress map." Nine categories, two hundred
items. It is the most useful artifact of its kind the constellation has
produced, because it does the one thing an honest backlog must do: it commits.

## the nine shelves

The hundred-item spine is organized as nine categories — a taxonomy of the
*preoccupation*, not of the deliverables:

- **A. Cognitive Systems** — entity simulation, cognition, evolution & selection
- **B. Physics** — particles, fields & forces
- **C. Environment** — terraforming, universe & the ocean
- **D. Civilization** — simulation, politics & society
- **E. Philosophy, Language & Ontology**
- **F. Hardware Prototypes**
- **G. Societal Systems**
- **H. Music, Language & Interface**
- **I. Cultural Artifacts**

Read the headings in order and you have the shape of a single research program
crossing cognition, physics, society, and culture — with hardware at the bottom
as the substrate that has to actually exist. Category B is where the
constellation's own vocabulary is sharpest: the *admissibility contract*
(`replayable ⇒ admissible`, item 41), the *minimal hypergraph syntax* — the
ordered wire `{-1, 0, +1}` (item 42), gauge duality, the RSVP plenum. Category A
carries the *breath loop* (`fetch → infer → learn → reinforce → repeat`,
item 9) that later surfaces as `honcho` memory.

## the cross-map — most of it already lives here

The honest move in the catalog is the **constellation cross-map**: a table that
admits that the WIP list is *not alien to the garden*. Item 1, Sentinel, is
`vaked-sentinel`, the fleet watchdogs. Items 50 and 60 are
`quantGame/galaxy.ts` and the celestial crates. Item 71 is the universal memory
kernel — `ultramesh-mem`, the memory loop. Item 72, "engines with seeds:
purpose as a parameter," is already `8b-is/alexiai` — the fully offline
sovereign app, ternary b1.58 substrate, loopback-only egress.

The un-mapped remainder is declared as *the garden's next season* — new seeds
waiting for first soil — and the constellation's rule applies to every one of
them, mapped or not: **deterministic, seeded, replayable — replayable ⇒
admissible.**

## why this belongs in a magazine

A catalog like this is usually invisible. It lives in a private note, ages, and
rots. The reason it is worth publishing — the reason it reads like a *state of
the art* and not a wish list — is that it refuses the two comfortable genres:
the roadmap (which promises) and the changelog (which only reports the
finished). It occupies the honest middle: **the named, statused, inspectable
present tense of a research program.**

The machinery that keeps it honest is not the list. It is the status column and
the cross-map. A claim that names its own mechanism — and its own residue — is
the readable thing. That is the subject of the next dispatch.
