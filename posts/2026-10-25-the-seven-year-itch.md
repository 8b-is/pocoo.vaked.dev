Twenty-four tracks from 2013, filed under electronic, all of them under one name — ten that Solee made alone, ten where Solee's name sits on somebody else's track as the remixer, two where the traffic runs the other way, and two slow-motion restatements of material already present. That ratio is the album's actual argument: a producer's portrait drawn as much by what he touches as by what he signs. — the title promises seven years; the running order counts them in credits rather than years.

## the read

Tracks 1–10 are the spine, and one of them, Home, is the only break in the solo line, shared with Slackwax. Then the record changes hands for ten straight tracks — Wankelmut & Emma Louise, Guy Mantzur & Dave Seaman, AKA AKA & Thalstroem, Oliver Lieb, Jules & Moss, Oliver Schories, Panik Pop with Joseph Westphal and NUSJA, Boss Axis, Jonas Woehl with Anna Leyne, Marc De Vole with Patrick Muschiol — and Solee is the interpreter each time, not the author. At 21 the direction reverses: Gabriel Ananda takes Platinum, Eelke Kleijn takes Sommerliebe. It closes on 23 and 24 with Caillou and Jonalu slowed rather than rebuilt — the same material at a different pulse, which is the quietest move on the whole thing.

So the shape is a loop. Ten originals, ten entries into other people's rooms, two returns, two retards. Nothing here is a new claim after track 20 — it is all circulation.
