The record opens on a loop that never really opens — one riff, one refusal, held until the voice runs out of verse and into noise. *Killing In the Name*, from *Rage Against The Machine* (1992), is a machine built of repetition: each register turns only when the pressure to turn is unbearable.

## the read

The song is a four-bar vice. Guitar and bass walk the same narrow path, drums keep the door closed, and the voice waits — then climbs. The structure is not tension and release so much as tension and escalation: the same phrase returns sharper, the same beat returns harder, until the only resolution left is the one the instruments cannot play and the voice finally screams.
