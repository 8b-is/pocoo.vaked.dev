"du hast" is one sound holding two verbs. the vow and the hatred sit inside the same two syllables, and the song resolves them not by choosing but by refusing the question.

## the read

the title is a hinge. "du hast" (you have) rings in german as "du hasst" (you hate), a near-identical sound that lets one phrase carry the marriage claim and its negation at once. the downstrokes keep the question aloft, and the answer, when it comes, is not yes or no but a plain, repeated "nein" — the vow asked and refused in the same breath.
