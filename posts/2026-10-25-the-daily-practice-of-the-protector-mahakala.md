## 1 · The Source of the Inner Amrita Offering (`OM MA DAK NÖ CHÜ TONG PAR JANG...`, Folios 8–11)

On Folios 8–11 of this 81-page pecha sits the exact **Inner Amrita Blessing and Offering Prayer** composed by the **8th Karmapa Mikyö Dorje (`མི་བསྐྱོད་རྡོ་རྗེ་`)** in response to the request of **Rinchen Palmo**:

```text
OM MA DAK NÖ CHÜ TONG PAR JANG
TONG PAY NGANG LAY YE SHE KHI
LUNG MAY TÖD GYE TENG NYID DU
THÖ PA SHA NGA DÜD TSI NGA
LUNG MAY KÖL WAY DAM TSIK PAY
DÜD TSI RAB BAR Ö NYI DE
YESHE DÜD TSI CHEN DRANG NAY
ER MAY OM DANG AH HUNG GYI
JIN LAB DRI RO TSÜNG MAY GYUR
...
KYE PAR CHÖ KYONG BER GYI NA ZA CHEN
PAL DEN LHA MO RANG JUNG GYAL MO NI
CHÖ CHAM KOR DANG CHAY PA CHÖ PAR GYI
```

Impure container and contents (`MA DAK NÖ CHÜ`, `Trit -1`) are purified into emptiness (`TONG PAR JANG`, `Trit 0`). Out of the state of emptiness, upon a hearth of wisdom wind and fire, a vast skullcup boils the Five Meats and Five Nectars into blazing light (`OM AH HUNG`, `Trit +1`), offered to the Root Guru (embodiment of all Yidams, Three Jewels, and Dharmapālas) and specifically to **Black-Cloaked Mahākāla Bernagchen (`CHÖ KYONG BER GYI NA ZA CHEN`)** and **Glorious Goddess Rangjung Gyalmo (`PAL DEN LHA MO RANG JUNG GYAL MO`)** together with **Namthöse**, **Dorje Lekpa**, **Sin Gön**, **Shing Kyong**, and **Khar Nak Dorje Gyalpo**.
