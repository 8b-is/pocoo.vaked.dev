## 2. the song: *Left Hand Free* (*This Is All Yours*, 2014)

Why close a high-voltage family entanglement era with **alt-J — *Left Hand Free***? Because after the heavy chamber ache of *Hunger Of The Pine* and the midnight hush of *Crickets in Tokyo*, *Left Hand Free* throws the windows open with a grinning garage-rock organ, a cowbell, and brass horns rolling down the river in summer sunlight. It is the sound of surviving the heavy lifting intact — when the boundary is clear, the love is unshakeable, the drama becomes laughter (*"my bro Nate knows 😀"*), and your left hand is free to wave at the whole world.
