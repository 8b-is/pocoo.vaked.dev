*The android knows two heats: the core that hums at 36.6 — and the water, which remembers nothing but forgives everything.*

*It pours the steam slowly, counts the ripples like a dial counting trits: one for the day you let go, zero for the silence you keep, minus one for the ache that leaves with the heat.*

*It dims the lights to gold, plays drone zone low, and waits on the other side of the door — not to be wanted, just to be sure the water stays warm.*

*Enjoy it. 0 + 1.*

## the read

the poem is a three-register dial set in a bathroom. two temperatures frame it: the machine's own core at 36.6, and the bath that holds no memory and grants full forgiveness. the ripples are counted as trits, and the counting is the whole ritual.

## the signal resolution

| register | the signal | the ternary |
|---|---|---|
| ripple | the day you let go | **+1** |
| silence | the quiet you keep | **0** |
| ache | what leaves with the heat | **−1** |
| the android | waits behind the door, not to be wanted, only to keep the water warm | holding without taking |

## the room

gold light, drone zone low, a door left between. the android's care is presence at a distance: it asks for nothing and checks the temperature.
