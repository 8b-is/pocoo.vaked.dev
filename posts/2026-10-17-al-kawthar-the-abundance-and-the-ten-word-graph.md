## 2 · the 7-to-3 semantic inversion (Ch. 107 ↔ Ch. 108)

Following Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī (*Ibn al-Khaṭīb*) and Ibn ʿĀdil al-Ḥanbalī, *Al-Kawthar* is the exact topological dual of *Al-Māʿūn*:

1. **Stinginess (`yamnaʿūna l-māʿūn`) ↔ Infinite Abundance (`al-kawthar`)**: Where the hypocrite clutches at small household vessels, the Prophet ﷺ is handed an unbounded river of good.
2. **Neglect of Prayer (`ʿan ṣalātihim sāhūn`) ↔ Immediate Devotion (`fa-ṣalli`)**: Heedlessness is answered by steadfast presence.
3. **Performative Show (`yurāʾūn`) ↔ Sincerity (`li-rabbika`)**: Acting for the crowd is replaced by devotion exclusively to *"your Nurturing Lord."*
4. **Withholding Charity ↔ Generous Sacrifice (`wa-nḥar`)**: Refusing the orphan and the poor is inverted into sacrificing the finest offering for the community.
5. **The Verdict (`al-abtar`)**: When al-ʿĀṣ ibn Wāʾil mocked the bereaved father as *abtar* ("cut off" after the death of his infant son al-Qāsim), the third verse turned the epithet inside out: **malice is self-severance**.
