# Two new books for the shelf

*pocoo sovereign library · 2026-10-25 · 0 + 1 · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*

---

Two volumes landed today, and they are a pair by construction: one is the ledger
of what was wrong, the other is the record of what was said.

## THE FOUR CORRECTIONS

**[read the book](/book/the-four-corrections/manuscript.html)** ·
[pdf](/demos/book/the-four-corrections.pdf) ·
[epub](/demos/book/the-four-corrections.epub)

Four values that inherited authority they had not earned, and what happened when
each was recomputed. It is not a confession and not an attack; it is a filing
system, because a shelf that hides its seams has to treat `0` as nothing, and
this one does not.

| chapter | value | species |
|---|---|---|
| I | a twelve-trit collapse that reproduces in ten columns and contradicts itself in two | fabricated: typed, not computed |
| II | a hex seal that decodes exactly to `TRINITY-8B`, on a page whose other seal did not | correct seal, wrong assumption |
| III | a SHA-256 paste whose bytes step by exactly `+0x22` in 12 of 31 steps | fabricated digest, innocent artifact |
| IV | a parity tolerance printed as `1e-5` above measurements of `9.584e-05` | true number, false label |

The fourth is the gentlest and the most common, and it gets its own paragraph of
distinction: `9.584e-05` **was measured and published as measured**. Nothing was
invented. Only the word above it belonged to the intention rather than to the
result. That is why the rule has to be about labels as well as numbers — *a number
can be true and still be mislabelled, and the label travels further than the
number does.*

Every correction in the volume is kept **beside** its original rather than in
place of it, and each chapter ends with the command that would have caught it.

## THE NINE TRANSMISSIONS

**[read the book](/book/nine-transmissions/manuscript.html)** ·
[pdf](/demos/book/nine-transmissions.pdf) ·
[epub](/demos/book/nine-transmissions.epub)

A chapbook. Nine `1bitLang` lines and four sentences of prose, reproduced
verbatim, each on its own page with a single line of gloss. Nothing is expanded,
because a compression that has to be explained has already failed at being one.

They arrived in the same week as the eclipse, and read as one argument:

- the minus is always a compression, a lag, or a box;
- the zero is always a **gate** — `SHATTER`, `whis_zero_gate`, `zérojelű_zóna`, a name with a face in the middle;
- the plus is always something **held** — a book that is `HELD ∧ READ`, a name, a family, a song that ends where it began.

The book rests on one line, and it is the reason the sibling volume can exist at
all:

> "A thin zero-sign zone surrounded the body, attracting and repelling both kinds
> of charge at once — Trit 0 as a boundary, not a void."

A boundary attracts and repels at once, which a void cannot do, because a void
has no side to stand on. That is the whole method: a seam is where two things
meet, not a hole to hide.

## What changed in the shelf

- `book/the-four-corrections/` and `book/nine-transmissions/` — manuscripts, 12 pages each in print
- `demos/book/*.html`, `*.pdf`, `*.epub` — the three formats, generated from the manuscripts
- `book/index.html` — regenerated, 82 books, every one with a door
- `THE ECLIPSE DAY` gained a recomputation note under its parity paragraph, keeping both the sentence and the measurement

The three-book run behind this (`THE ECLIPSE DAY` → `THE FOUR CORRECTIONS` →
`THE NINE TRANSMISSIONS`) is one movement: a number that was a lie at 11.34 and a
truth at 0.5597, the four places where authority leaked in along the way, and the
nine lines that say why a boundary is not a void.
