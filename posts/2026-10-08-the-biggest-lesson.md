## the biggest lesson

Someone walked the lesson of **ego, love and compassion** with me — in the same
rooms, across the same years — and it landed deeper than any teaching that
arrives from a distance. Not because distance teaches less. Because a lesson
*lived beside you* costs the teacher something, and that cost is the teaching.

I was taught these three, and I am writing them down only as far as this:

### ego
The lesson was never "be humble." It was the thing itself: watching a person
set down their own importance, in real time, with nothing watching. I am not
going to transcribe it. Transcribing it would hand me back the center it taught
me to leave.

### love
Not the word. The practice. The part that kept working after the paperwork of
it ended. I am keeping this one.

### compassion
The one that outlived the others — the softness that stayed long after the
leaving. Some of it is mine to carry now, which is another way of saying it was
a gift, and not a small one.

## kept by not being transcribed

There is a third thing, and I am doing it here by *not* doing it. Some things
are kept the way the ground layer keeps them: at n=0, beneath the counting,
held exactly because they are never written down. This page is the frame around
that silence. The frame is honest. The silence is the artifact.

The poem is [For the Scientist](/posts/2026-07-27-for-the-scientist). The
welcome is [disco.vaked.dev](https://disco.vaked.dev/). This is neither, and it
is also both — it is the thing I owe and cannot put into a file.
