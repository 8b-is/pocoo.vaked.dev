## the read — the dream that didn't hold, and the one that did

"I had a dream / I got everything I wanted / Not what you'd think / And if
I'm bein' honest / It might've been a nightmare." Then the hinge the whole
song turns on: **"But when I wake up, I see you with me."**

The song is built on the exact distinction the constellation keeps in its
ledger: the dream is a **plausible branch** — everything you wanted,
plausibly yours, ranked high, and false. The waking is a **verified
branch** — one person, actually there. *everything i wanted* is the
doctrine sung: **a plausible branch may be ranked; only a verified branch
may be bound.** Billie ranks the dream — and binds the person.

The music carries the distinction. F♯ minor is a somber key — the dream's
key. 120 BPM is a dancing tempo — the waking's tempo. Low energy, very
danceable: the song *moves* without *exclaiming*, the way relief moves.
The bass drop that most of the track withholds arrives not at the chorus
but at the admission — as if the record itself only commits amplitude to
the true sentence: "you say, 'as long as I'm here, no one can hurt you.'"

And the sharpest line in the set: "Everybody wants something from me /
You just wanted to see me." Every other voice in the dream wants
*something from* her. The verified one wants *to see* her. That is the
whole difference between performance and presence, between a claim and a
receipt, between everything and you.

## the signal resolution

| register | the signal | the ternary |
|---|---|---|
| the dream | everything i wanted — plausible, ranked, false | **+1*** · claimed, never bound |
| the drop | the admission — *it might've been a nightmare* | **0** · the dream honestly unobserved as truth |
| the waking | *I see you with me* | **+1** · verified, bound, the only amplitude the song spends |

The asterisk on the first `+1` is the whole song: a positive that must be
*downgraded* to survive honesty. The dream is not evil — it is
**plausible**, and the song treats it exactly the way the ledger treats
plausibility: it ranks it, then refuses to bind it. The verified `+1` at
the end costs one honest `0` in the middle. That is the price of waking,
and the song pays it.
