## I. Programmable Material Organization — why physical shape is an illusion

In **[Programmable Material Organization: Process History, Structural Constraint, and Functional Manufacturing](/papers/flyxion-trilogy-2026/trilogy-iii/programmable_material_organization.pdf)** (`standardgalactic/laboratory`, `4c68f3c`), Flyxion dismantles the conventional account of digital fabrication:

> Two objects that are indistinguishable by external geometric measurement $G(O_1) = G(O_2)$ may differ in their internal organization $\mathcal{S} = \mathcal{P}(u(t), e(t))$ sufficiently to behave in opposite ways: $F(\mathcal{S}_1) = -F(\mathcal{S}_2)$.

- **Smectic liquid-crystalline elastomers (LCEs)** extruded along identical toolpaths switch the sign of their actuation strain—contracting or elongating—solely according to the nozzle temperature and shear history frozen into their director field during deposition.
- **Meter-scale metallic single crystals** grown via wire-arc deposition with pulsed remelting preserve crystallographic continuity across repeated nucleation hazards through thermal gradient control rather than geometric slicing.
- **Task-relative sufficiency:** A compressed manufacturing record $R = \Pi(u(t), e(t))$ is sufficient for functional observable $F$ at tolerance $\varepsilon$ if and only if $I(F(\mathcal{S}); (u,e) \mid R) \le \varepsilon$.

In the house language of `MEM|16-10`: **identity is continuity evidence, not external contour.**

## II. Compression Without Verification — lock/fork continuation geometry & safety

In **[Compression Without Verification: Self-Distillation and the Reshaping of Continuation Geometry](/papers/flyxion-trilogy-2026/trilogy-iii/compression_without_verification.pdf)** (`standardgalactic/laboratory`, `4c68f3c` & `standardgalactic/admissibility-lab`, `3358fb4`), Flyxion examines why fine-tuning a coding model on its own unverified, temperature-sampled outputs (Simple Self-Distillation, SSD) yields large pass@1 gains even when truncation removal renders most training outputs incomplete as programs:

1. **Lock sharpening vs. fork preservation:** Self-distillation does not inject new certified knowledge; it reorganizes *continuation geometry*—concentrating probability mass at **lock** positions (where local syntax or invariants fix the continuation) while preserving competing branches at **fork** positions under shifted decoding policies.
2. **Why compressed history breaks AI safety:** When an agent's history or chain-of-thought is compressed into a summary state $\tilde{h}_t = C(h_{1:t})$ without an explicit verifier, **admissibility is non-conservative under compression**. A trace that is inadmissible in full ($h_{1:t}$) can pass a gate after lossy summarization drops provenance taint—and restoring a prior model checkpoint never undoes consequences produced in the world while the model was deployed.

This is the mathematical proof of the Sovereign Library's first rule: *"A plausible branch may be ranked; only a verified branch may be bound."*

## III. Adversaria (02 & 11) — routing is not adjudication & the two-plane ledger

In **[02: Routing Is Not Adjudication](/papers/flyxion-trilogy-2026/trilogy-iii/routing_is_not_adjudication.pdf)** and **[11: Inscription and Authority](/papers/flyxion-trilogy-2026/trilogy-iii/inscription_and_authority.pdf)** (`standardgalactic/adversaria`, `df7cfa7`), Flyxion specifies a five-stage ledger pipeline that separates the **Authorization Plane** from the **Argument Plane**:

$$\text{Inscribe} \longrightarrow \text{Validate} \longrightarrow \text{Admit} \longrightarrow \text{Project} \longrightarrow \text{Activate}$$

- **Open Inscription Theorem:** For every finite closed set of records with consistent identifiers and acyclic references, the set is a valid ledger under *every* policy, whatever gates the policy contains. Validity never mentions policy.
- **Stored $\neq$ Admitted $\neq$ Endorsed:** Inscription depends only on the record and returns a cryptographic receipt. Authority governs only a *projection* onto a surface across seven dispositions (`admitted`, `duplicate`, `malformed`, `unscoped`, `quarantined`, `gated`, `refused`).
- **Routing invariance:** Once the active set of admitted records is projected, argument status (`IN`, `OUT`, `UND`) depends strictly on the active set and argument references—never on routing weights, view counts, reputation, or controversy flags.
