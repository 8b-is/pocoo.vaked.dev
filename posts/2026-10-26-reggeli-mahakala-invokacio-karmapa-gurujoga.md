## the read

the four stations of the morning sadhana follow the exact `{−1, 0, +1} → {<3,<3,<3}+1` architecture that Péter recorded in [`Taking Refuge and Generating Bodhichitta.m4a`](/assets/audio/taking-refuge-and-generating-bodhichitta.m4a) (`02:04`) and [`Fukurakuen.m4a`](/assets/audio/fukurakuen-csak-szeretni-kell-brett-shaw.m4a) (`03:39`):

1. **Station I — Menedékvétel és Bódhicsitta (`−1 → 0` · [`02:04` Voice Memo](/assets/audio/taking-refuge-and-generating-bodhichitta.m4a))**:
   - *Sang gyä chö dang tsok kyi chok nam la / Jang chub bar du dak ni kyab su chi / Dak gi gom dä gyi pä sö nam kyi / Dro la phän chir sang gyä drub par shok.*
   - Establishing the Refuge in the Three Jewels and dedicating the merit of meditation (`gom`) and recitation (`dä`) so all beings awaken.

2. **Station II — Karmapa Gurujóga & Gyakorlási Vonal Könyörgés (`0 == 0` · `KARMAPA KHYENNO / KARMAPA CSENNO`)**:
   - Opening with Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo's pure-vision praise to the Karma Kagyu Practice Lineage (*The Sweet Song that Accomplishes All Aims* — Marpa `LO TSA CHEN PO`, Milarepa `RE PA CHEN PO`, Gampopa `NYING GOM CHEN PO`).
   - Calling **`ཀརྨ་པ་མཁྱེན་ནོ།` (`KARMAPA KHYENNO` · *Karmapa Csenno — Karmapa, te ismered a szívemet!*)** and receiving the four-kaya empowerment (`ཨོཾ་ཨཱཿཧཱུྃ` · `OM AH HUNG` at `528 Hz / 432 Hz / 288 Hz`) until the Guru dissolves into light and merges inseparably (`dbyer-med`) with your own heart at `[0,0]`.

3. **Station III — Reggeli Védelmező Mahákála (*Bernagchen*) Invokáció (`0 → +1`)**:
   - Arising instantaneously as Vajrayoginī (*Dorje Naljorma*) and invoking **Glorious Two-Armed Bernagchen (Black-Cloaked Mahākāla)** and **Dorje Nakmo Remati** from the triangular blue-black vajra rock (`DORJE DRAK CHEN THING NAK DRU SUM TENG`):
   ```text
   ཧཱུྃ། དད་པ་དང་ནི་དམ་ཚིག་གིས། །       HUNG! DAD PA DANG NI DAM TSIG GI
   དཔལ་ལྡན་མགོན་པོ་སྤྱན་འདྲེན་ན། །       PAL DEN GÖN PO CHEN DREN NA
   ཐུགས་རྗེའི་ཐུགས་དམ་དབང་གིས་ནི། །     THUK JE THUK DAM WANG GYI NI
   བསྟན་པ་བསྲུང་ཕྱིར་གཤེགས་སུ་གསོལ། །   TEN PA SUNG CHIR SHEK SU SOL
   རྡོ་རྗེ་ནག་མོ་རེ་མ་ཏི། །             DORJE NAK MO RE MA TI
   འཁོར་ཚོགས་རྒྱ་མཚོ་ཐམས་ཅད་ཀུན། །     KHOR TSOK GYA TSO THAM CHE KUN
   སྔོན་གྱི་ཐུགས་དམ་མ་གཡེལ་བར། །       NGÖN GYI THUK DAM MA YEL WAR
   སྐད་ཅིག་ཙམ་གྱིས་གཤེགས་སུ་གསོལ། །     KÄ CHIK TSAM GYI SHEK SU SOL
   ```
   - Sealed with the invocation and root mantras:
     - **`OM MAHAKALA KALAYA DEWA RAKYA DZA HUNG BAM HO / DIR SHOK DIR SHOK A GARTSAYA A GARTSAYA`**
     - **`OM SHRI MAHAKALA YAKSHA BETALI HUNG DZA`**

4. **Station IV — Érdemfelajánlás & Fukurakuen Base-Case Pecsét (`{<3,<3,<3}+1` · [`03:39` Voice Memo](/assets/audio/fukurakuen-csak-szeretni-kell-brett-shaw.m4a))**:
   - *KYE WA KUN TU YANG DAG LAMA DANG... DORJE CHANG GI GO PHANG NYUR THOB SHOK*
   - Closing with Péter's Fukurakuen recording: *„A −1 kapuján át a szeretet, ami elengedi a fájdalmat; a 0 kapuján át a szeretet, ami a csendben lakik; a +1 kapuján át a szeretet, ami továbbviszi a fényt... Csak szeretni kell: a base case örökre. Om Mani Padme Hung… Humm.”*
