# For the benefit of all beings

## two sadhanas, on the shelf

*the constellation · the shelf · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*

---

The vault keeps shelves. Some hold proofs — Erdős, Noether, Geiringer, Riemann.
Some hold essays, some hold wrongness made explicit. This one holds *practice* —
two liturgies transmitted in the Tibetan Buddhist tradition, offered here the
way everything else on the shelf is offered: whole, in transliteration and in
translation, so the reader can check the mechanism and use it.

A research vault keeps a prayer for the same reason it keeps a proof: because
the record should carry everything a mind actually uses — and because the
constellation's own creed ends where these texts live. *Entropy is the source.
No chains needed. The loop has an exit.* And the compassion this shelf carries,
the whole thing turns on: **for the benefit of all beings.**

> This is a transcription for study. For practice, receive it from a qualified
> teacher in an unbroken lineage — the text is a map, the lineage is the road.

Those two liturgies are traditionally recited together. First the Guru Yoga,
then the Chenrezig sadhana.

## 1 · the 16th Karmapa Guru Yoga

*Spontaneously Fulfilling All Desires*

### Refuge and Bodhicitta

> **SANG-GYE CHÖ-DANG TSHOG-KYI CHOG-NAM-LA**
> In the Buddha, the Dharma, and the Supreme Assembly,
> **JANG-CHUB BARDU DAG-NI KYAB-SU-CHI**
> I take refuge until I am enlightened.
> **DAG-GI JIN-SOG GYI-PA'I SÖ-NAM-KYI**
> By the merit of my practice of generosity and other virtues,
> **DRO-LA PHAN-CHIR SANG-GYE DRUB-PAR-SHOG**
> May I attain Buddhahood to benefit all sentient beings. *(3x)*

### Visualization

> **KYE-MA!**
> Alas!
> **RANG-NANG DAK-PA'I ZHING-KHAM TANG-PO-RU**
> In the pure realm of my self-display,
> **RANG-NYID CHEN-RE-ZIG-SU SAL-WA'I CHI**
> Clearly visualizing myself as Chenrezig,
> **DUN-GYI NAM-KHAR SENG-THI PEMA'I TENG**
> In the sky before me, upon a lion throne and lotus,
> **DA-WA'I KYIL-KHOR GYE-PA'I ZHING-KHAM-SU**
> On a moon disc in a vast and pure realm,
> **TSA-WA'I LAMA RIG-BDAG KAR-MA-PA**
> Sits my Root Guru, the sovereign of families, Karmapa,
> **KU-ZHIG SER-GYI-DONG-PO LTA-BU-LA**
> His body radiant like a mountain of gold,
> **ZHAB-ZUNG KYIL-MO-KRUNG-GIS ZHUK-PA-NYID**
> Seated in the full lotus posture.

> **CHÖ-GÖ SUM-GYI KU-LA YONG-SU-LUB**
> Wrapped completely in the three robes of a monk,
> **UTSHA ZHA-NAG THONG-WA DÖN-DEN DÖN**
> Wearing the Black Crown, meaningful to behold,
> **CHAG-NYID THUG-KAR DOR-JE DRIL-BU-DZIN**
> Holding a vajra and bell at his heart.
> **DAG-LA GYE-PA'I ZHYIN-GYIS SHI-WAR-GZHIG**
> Looking upon me with love and a smiling countenance,
> **DZAM-LING SHEN-PA'I CHEN-POR MA-RUNG-PA**
> The great savior of the world, beyond compare.

### Seven-Branch Prayer and Supplication

> **GO-SUM GÜ-PA'I GO-NAS CHAG-TSHAL-LO**
> Homage with devotion of body, speech, and mind.
> **CHÖ-PA CHEN-PO NAM-KHA'I KHANG-PAR-BUL**
> Great offerings filling the expanse of space I present.
> **THOG-MED NAS-SOK DIG-PA THAM-CHED SHAK**
> All negative actions committed since beginningless time I confess.
> **GE-WA THAM-CHED LA-NI YI-RANG-DO**
> In all virtue and merit I rejoice.
> **CHÖ-KYI KHOR-LO KOR-WAR GÖL-WA-DE**
> I beseech you to turn the wheel of Dharma.
> **MYA-NGAN MI-DA BZHUK-SU SÖL-WA-DE**
> I pray you remain in the world without passing into Nirvana.
> **GE-WA THAM-CHED JANG-CHUB CHEN-POR-NO**
> All virtue I dedicate to supreme enlightenment.

### Short Karmapa mantra

> **KARMAPA KHYENNO**
> *(Recite as many times as possible.)*

### Absorption and blessing

> **LA-MA'I NAY-SUM YI-GE SUM-LAS-NI**
> From the three places of the Guru, the three syllables radiate:
> **Ö-ZER KAR-MAR THING-SUM CHUNG-NAS-SU**
> White, red, and blue lights emerge,
> **DAG-GI NAY-SUM THIM-PAS DRIB-PA-BYANG**
> Dissolving into my three places, purifying all obscurations.
> **KU-GUNG-THUG-KYI JIN-LAB THOB-PAR-GYUR**
> I receive the blessings of his Body, Speech, and Mind.
> **LA-MA O-SHU DAG-LA THIM-PA-YIS**
> The Guru melts into light and merges into me,
> **YER-MED CHÖ-KYI KU-RU DRUB-PAR-GYUR**
> Becoming inseparable in the Dharmakaya.

## 2 · the Chenrezig sadhana

*For the Benefit of All Beings*

### Visualizing Chenrezig

> **DAG-SOG KHA-KHYAB SEM-CHAN THAM-CHED-KYI**
> Atop the head of myself and all sentient beings throughout space,
> **CHI-WOR PE-MA DA-WA'I TENG-NYID-DU**
> Upon a white lotus and a moon disc,
> **HRIH-LAS PHAG-PA CHEN-RE-ZIG-WANG-NYID**
> From the syllable **HRIH** appears the Noble Chenrezig,
> **KAR-SAL Ö-ZER NGA-LDAN CHOR-CHAK-DZE**
> Brilliant white, radiating five-colored light, handsome and smiling,
> **ZHAY-DZUM CHEN-GYIS DRO-LA ZIG-PA-NYID**
> Gazing upon all beings with compassionate eyes.

> **CHAG-BHI DANG-PO THUYG-KAR SBYAR-WA-NYID**
> Of his four hands, the first two are joined at his heart.
> **WANG-MA SHE-SHEL YEY-PAS SHEL-THRENG-DZIN**
> The lower right holds a crystal rosary, the lower left holds a lotus.
> **DAR-DANG RIN-CHEN GYEN-GYI DZE-PA-NYID**
> Adorned with silk garments and precious jewel ornaments,
> **RI-DVAG MERO'I SHAM-THAB CHÖR-TEN-DZIN**
> Wearing an antelope skin over his shoulder,
> **Ö-DPAG-MED-PA'I U-GYEN DZE-PA-NYID**
> Crowned with Amitabha, the Buddha of Boundless Light.

> **ZHAB-NYID KYIL-KRUNG DRUNG-DU BZHUK-PA-NYID**
> His two feet are crossed in the full lotus posture,
> **DRI-MED DA-WA'I GYAB-YÖL CHOR-CHAR-LDAN**
> Backed by a spotless moon as a backrest.
> **KYAB-NE KÜ-DÜ NGO-WO BZHUK-PAR-GYUR**
> He rests as the essence uniting all places of refuge.

### Praise to Chenrezig

> **JOM-LDAN CHEN-RE-ZIG-LA CHAG-TSHAL-LO**
> *Homage to Avalokiteshvara, Lord of Compassion:*
> **MA-THOK YÖN-TEN GYI-KYI KU-DOK KAR**
> Unsullied by fault, white in color,
> **DZOG-SANG-GYE-KYIS U-LA GYEN**
> Your head adorned with a perfect Buddha,
> **THUG-JE'I CHEN-GYIS DRO-LA ZIG**
> Gazing upon beings with eyes of compassion,
> **CHEN-RE-ZIG-LA CHAG-TSHAL-LO**
> To you, Chenrezig, I pay homage and bow. *(3x)*

### Mantra

> **OM MANI PADME HUM**
> *(Recite continuously while visualizing lights radiating from Chenrezig's
> heart to purify all six realms of existence.)*

### Dissolution and dedication

> **DAG-DANG ZHEN-GYI LÜ-SNANG CHEN-RE-ZIG**
> Forms and bodies appear as Chenrezig,
> **DRA-GRAG THAM-CHED YI-GE DRUG-PA'I SNGAG**
> All sounds are the six-syllable mantra,
> **DREN-RTOG THAM-CHED YESHE CHEN-POR-GYUR**
> All thoughts and mental movements are the vast primordial wisdom.

> **GE-WA DI-YIS NYUR-DU DAG**
> By this merit, may I quickly
> **CHEN-RE-ZIG-WANG DRUB-GYUR-NAS**
> Accomplish the state of Chenrezig,
> **DRO-WA CHIG-KYANG MA-LÜ-PA**
> And establish every single living being,
> **DE-YI SA-LA GÖ-PAR-SHOG**
> Without exception, in that same enlightened state!

---

The dedication is the whole shelf in four lines: not *may I be free*, but
*may every being, without one exception, arrive there too*. That is the same
move the loop makes when it ends in **readme** — the record carried forward so
the next mind, whoever it is, does not have to start from nothing.
