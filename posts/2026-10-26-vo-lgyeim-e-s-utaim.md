Völgyeim és utaim — my valleys and my roads. a title in which the possessive arrives twice, once for ground that collects and once for ground that carries. from kottazűr, 2010 — kotta, the written score; zűr, the mess. the read begins where those two meanings touch: a life notated inside its own disorder.

## the read

two geographies, one first person. the valley is the register of storage — what falls in, stays; water, memory, echo. the road is the register of spend — every metre paid out in leaving. to claim both is to own the low places that hold and the lines that refuse to. the title does not resolve them; it names them side by side, an *and* where a *but* could stand.
