## the read

*GLUE* (Belfast duo Matt McBriar and Andy Ferguson, 2017) has no verses, only a wordless vocal loop floating above a 2-step garage breakbeat and a synth pad that swells like chest-warmth. Why call an instrumental *GLUE*? Because in a room full of separate frequencies, rhythm is what binds the vertices together without forcing them to be identical.
