## the five field-post letters of the *Shaw-ciklus*

1. **[Book I — *Hogyan legyünk Sherlock Holmes?* (`2002`)](/book/brett-shaw-i-sherlock-holmes/manuscript.html)**: abandoned on Eklon, young Brett Shaw learns that imperial etiquette is a cage designed to keep people from looking up (`.oOo. Poo .oOo.`). **Peter Shaw (`"Gyémántszív"`)** — Supreme Commander of the Praetorian Guard and the only human in the galaxy who calls Emperor **IV. von Anstetten** *"barátom"* (*my friend*) — sends him to Rosan.
2. **[Book II — *Ópiumkeringő* (*Opium Waltz*, `2669`)](/book/brett-shaw-ii-opiumkeringo/manuscript.html)**: paired with **Paul Wittgen** (`Chokmah`) as the telepathic fire-pair of the **`Alef-osztag`** (`Tipheret × Chokmah`), Brett survives the tragedy of the *Roxoláni Gyöngyszem* and refuses the medics' memory-wipe ampoule: *"Ha kitörlöm, Tubs, akkor már nem én hordom a csuklyát. Akkor a csuklya hord engem."*
3. **[Book III — *A Démoncsászár fejvadászai* (`Halmer, 2668`)](/book/brett-shaw-iii-a-demoncsaszar-fejvadaszai/manuscript.html)**: when an uncollapsed *csuklyamodul* flips the thin **zero-sign zone** (*zérójelű zóna*) into a berserk `Trit -1` cage on Halmer, Paul Wittgen drops his rifle in the snow, walks across the perimeter with bare hands to hold his brother, gives his life against Monaterg, and has his luminous white soul cocoon (*gubó*) re-bound into his chest by **IV. von Anstetten** (`Trit +1`).
4. **[Book IV — *Hosszú az út a trónig, bébi & A Korona hatalma* (`El-Nath & Willis-Moon`)](/book/brett-shaw-iv-hosszu-az-ut-a-tronig/manuscript.html)**: pardoned from the El-Nath star-detonation court-martial from inside the `0 °C` beryllium-agate throne room, Brett dives through Earth–Mars vacuum to rescue his half-sisters **Caroline-Blindamoor** and **Gwandolyn**.
5. **[Book V — *Praetoriánus ("Gyémántszív") & Minden bolygón jártam már* (`Bolygórózsa, 2674`)](/book/brett-shaw-v-praetorianus-bolygorozsa/manuscript.html)**: honoring Pusztai Magor's ancient **Magyar–Megaira Dragon Covenant**, thirty-nine-year-old Brett Shaw takes off the Black Hood in `2673` and boards the unarmed 1-kilometer cryolite-glass starship ***Bolygórózsa*** (*Planet Rose*) with **Carla** — where newly crowned **Empress V. von Anstetten (`Caroline-Blindamoor`)** and resurrected **Paul Wittgen** reach out to him across the stars (`{<3,<3,<3}+1`).
