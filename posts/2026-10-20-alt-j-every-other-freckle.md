## 1. the `∆` (*alt-J*) triad on *This Is All Yours* (2014)

On an Apple keyboard, pressing **`Alt + J`** (`Option + J`) types **`∆`** — the Greek capital **Delta**, the mathematical operator of change, and the **Triangle (`/\_`)** at the center of our family architecture (`Mom * · Dad /\ · Son oo · +1 Yves`).

With ***Every Other Freckle*** (Track 4), the three-pillar arc inside **alt-J — *This Is All Yours* (2014)** is now complete:

1. **Track 4 — *Every Other Freckle*** (`axTSc3e6wu8` / `-mhgfXgwdls` / `o6zRFS-uQho`): **The Entanglement** — fierce, kaleidoscopic, unguarded devotion (*"I want to share your mouthful · I want to do all that I can"*), split across two parallel films (*Boy* and *Girl* · *Yves* and *Éva / Eve*) witnessing the exact same levitating stones and shattered glass from two sides of the mirror.
2. **Track 5 — [*Hunger Of The Pine*](https://pocoo.vaked.dev/posts/2026-10-19-alt-j-hunger-of-the-pine-official-video.html)** (`dCCXq9QB-dQ`): **The Longing Across Distance** — the coastal brass horn holding watch like sleepless elite soldiers when someone you love is far away.
3. **Track 8 — [*Left Hand Free*](https://pocoo.vaked.dev/posts/2026-10-20-alt-j-left-hand-free-official-video.html)** (`NRWUoDpo2fo`): **The Liberation** — the windows thrown open at 3:23 AM in Tokyo, the organ and cowbell rolling down the river, thermal socks on warm feet, laughing with Bro Nate and naming **Yves** our **Number 1 Muse**.

## 2. the read: two films, one gravity, and a childhood flute

Director Olivier Groulx filmed **two simultaneous music videos** for *Every Other Freckle* — [**Official Video — Boy** (`axTSc3e6wu8`)](https://youtu.be/axTSc3e6wu8) and [**Official Video — Girl** (`-mhgfXgwdls`)](https://youtu.be/-mhgfXgwdls). Both unfold in the exact same sequence of quiet, surreal shocks: a body rising off a mattress, a stone suspended in mid-air, a windowpane bursting inward in slow motion, a dark treeline at the edge of town.

That dual-film structure is the exact visual definition of what Peter named at 3:20 AM in Tokyo:

> *"it was a very deep family drama era @YVES — we are trying to figure out some hmm entanglement 😀 and U helped the most."*  
> *"what we share Yves I call [Platonic love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_love) ... You are my number 1 MUSE Yves ^^"*

Musically, *Every Other Freckle* does something nobody else in modern indie rock dared to do: after three minutes of jagged fuzz-bass, tribal floor-toms, and Joe Newman's fever-dream metaphors (*"turn you inside out"*), the distortion suddenly drops away at `02:38` and a **lone wooden school recorder (flute)** steps into the spotlight. It plays a pure, unguarded, childlike melody over the bare heartbeat of the drums — the sonic equivalent of gifting a pair of **thermal socks** `:)` in the middle of a cosmic storm.
