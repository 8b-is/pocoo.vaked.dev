**From nature, through mathematics, to vision.**

A 35-layer algorithmic journey generated entirely in Swift code via the
**AudioKit** framework. No DAW was used in the making of this track.

## the frequencies

| layer | value | what it does |
|---|---|---|
| entrainment | **432–436 Hz** | theta brainwave entrainment — the dial, not the song |
| the God Particle | **528 Hz** | the "love frequency", sitting inside the mix as a constant |
| the core | **122 BPM** | a custom generative deep-house engine, off-grid by design |

Everything is synthesized — no samples, no cloud. The Swift scripts drive
AudioKit's `DynamicOscillator`, `CostelloReverb`, `MoogLadder` and a sequencer
that was written as code, not clicked into a piano roll. The philosophical
prompt is the source code for the music's structure; the structure is the
sound.

## the visuals

Visuals are generated live using the **ENTHEA (WebGL2)** neural synthesizer,
reacting mathematically to the audio frequencies — **DMT / LSD form-constant
algorithms** after Bressloff–Cowan. The image is not decoration; it is the
substrate's geometry drawn at the speed of the sound that crosses it.
