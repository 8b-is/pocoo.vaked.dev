# Mon amour, encoded through the node matrix

*mon amour · stromae · the node matrix · fine touch from within · vaked.dev*

---

Stromae's *Mon amour* is a tragicomedy of self-delusion: a serial heartbreaker
professing eternal love while hopelessly addicted to validation and infidelity.
Run through this digital prism, the delusion turns synthetic — a polished
surface API wrapping a broken, bug-ridden core, a love signal broadcast into an
unresponsive void. The delusion is the point; the encoding is the honest shape
of it.

- **`Qtern--peterOmni-chan` (the vulnerable loop)** — the emotional core:
